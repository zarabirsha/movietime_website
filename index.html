What is MovieTime?+

MovieTime is an app for shared movie watching, built around synchronized playback and personal audio and subtitle controls.

Can everyone use different subtitles?+

Yes. Each person can choose their own subtitles and audio from the tracks available in the movie, without changing anyone else's preferences.

Is the player above the real app?+

It's an interactive interface concept, not a live MovieTime session. Try pausing the preview or switching on subtitles for a taste of the experience.

Where can I download MovieTime?+

MovieTime is available on the App Store. Follow the link to download the app and check device compatibility.
